# Product Management and Technical Writing in Research Delivery

Applied Creole Linguistics uses product-management practices to prioritize research questions, define small milestones, maintain explicit assumptions and risks, and state acceptance criteria. Technical-writing practices make methods, sources, uncertainty, consent, and results understandable and reproducible.

The shared portfolio operating model also defines boundaries with BabelGlobalKreole (framework) and the Mascarene Knowledge Commons (public discovery). Research hypotheses, tested observations, and validated findings must remain clearly distinguished.

See [Product Management and Technical Writing Operating Model](https://github.com/Francois-EF-WebOps/BabelGlobalKreole/blob/main/docs/product-management-and-technical-writing-operating-model.md) and the [Convergence Research and Validation Plan](docs/convergence-research-and-validation-plan.md).

This documents intended practice; it does not claim that all tests or participant validation have already occurred.
